# What this is built on

Archet is an implementation of published work, not an invention. Every
mechanism in the model and every calibrated figure comes from somewhere,
and this file says where, so a reader can check the code against the
source it claims to follow. The source cites these beside the constants
they set.

Bowed string
- M. Demoucron, On the control of virtual violins, thesis, IRCAM / KTH,
  2008: the modal string with a one-point friction interaction and the
  bridge-force output.
- Friedlander; McIntyre, Schumacher and Woodhouse: the graphical solution
  of the friction interaction.
- J. C. Schelleng, The bowed string and the player, JASA 1973: the window
  of bow force in which the string sustains Helmholtz motion.
- A. Askenfelt, Measurement of the bowing parameters in violin playing,
  JASA 1986 and 1989.
- E. Schoonderwaldt, Mechanics and acoustics of violin bowing, thesis,
  KTH, 2009.
- J. Meyer, Acoustics and the Performance of Music: the dynamic range of
  the violin.
- K. Guettler, On the creation of the Helmholtz motion in bowed strings,
  Acustica 2002.
- S. Ternstroem, and R. Cuesta and P. Chandna: the pitch scatter of a
  section.
- R. Rasch, Synchronization in performed ensemble music, Acustica 1979:
  onset asynchrony between players (used by the scored-piece harness).

Plucked string
- D. Chadefaux, J.-L. Le Carrou and B. Fabre, Experimentally-based
  description of harp plucking, JASA 2012: the movement of the plucking
  point and the pluck as a release.
- C. Traube, An interdisciplinary study of the timbre of the classical
  guitar, thesis, McGill, 2004: plucking-point estimation from spectra.
- N. H. Fletcher and T. D. Rossing, The Physics of Musical Instruments:
  string lengths and tunings.

Bodies
- J. Woodhouse, The acoustics of the violin: a review, Reports on
  Progress in Physics 2014: modal overlap above the signature modes.
- H. Duennwald: the frequency bands of violin quality.
- G. Bissinger: the damping of violin body modes.
- A. Askenfelt, Eigenmodes and tone quality of the double bass, STL-QPSR
  1982, and his chapter in The Science of String Instruments (Rossing
  ed., 2010).
- E. Bynum and T. D. Rossing, the cello chapter of the same book; I. M.
  Firth, 1974, for its mode damping.
- D. Coffey, 2013, and A. Powell, UC Davis REU report: viola modes.

Recordings the model was calibrated on (none is redistributed here)
- University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios, Musical Instrument
  Samples, Lawrence Fritts: anechoic violin, viola, cello and double bass,
  plucked and bowed, freely usable.
- Philharmonia Orchestra sound samples: violin long notes with vibrato,
  free to use.
